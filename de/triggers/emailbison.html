Emailbison

Available Emailbison triggers for automating workflows

Email Bison triggers in AptlyStar start workflows when outbound email events occur — sends, opens, bounces, replies, unsubscribes, tag changes, and mailbox health updates.

Use these triggers to route hot replies to sales, pause sequences on bounces, sync CRM records when leads engage, and alert ops when sending accounts disconnect.

Emailbison provides 17 triggers for automating workflows based on events.
