4:Td0b,# Get Row

`GET /api/v1/tables/{tableId}/rows/{rowId}`

## Description
Retrieve a single row by its ID.

## Parameters

- **tableId** (path) (required) — `string`: The unique identifier of the table.

- **rowId** (path) (required) — `string`: The unique identifier of the row.

- **workspaceId** (query) (required) — `string`: The unique identifier of the workspace.
