`GET /api/v1/tables/{tableId}`

## Description
Retrieve a table's metadata, schema, and row count.

## Parameters

- **tableId** (path) (required) — `string`: The unique identifier of the table.

- **workspaceId** (query) (required) — `string`: The unique identifier of the workspace.
